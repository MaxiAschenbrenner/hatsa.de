You probably know the game from long drives: find a license plate with the number 1, then one with a 2, then 3, all the way to 9999. Tag Turtle keeps track of the number you're looking for, on your own or together with family and friends. The app is still in development and not yet on the App Store.

## Features

- **The number you're after:** It's shown big, as a license plate, and tapping "Seen" moves you on to the next one.
- **No account, no signal needed:** Playing solo works right away, without signing up, even on back roads with no reception.
- **Teams with one shared counter:** Everyone hunts for the same number, and whoever spots it first taps. The others see it instantly.
- **Invite with a code:** Others join with a six-character code or a link, and you can be in several teams at once.
- **History and undo:** "Recently seen" shows who counted which number and when, and a mistaken tap can be taken back.
- **Join mid-game:** Already playing? Set the number you're currently on and carry on from there.

## How it works

1. On the road, watch for a license plate with the number the app shows, for example M AB 1.
2. When you spot one, tap the green button. Then it's on to the next number.
3. To play as a team, create an account and a team, then send the code to the others.
4. At 10, 25, 50 and every hundred after that, you get confetti.

## FAQ

**Do I need an account?**\
No. Solo play works without one. You only need an account for teams, or to keep your progress the same across devices. When you sign up, your current progress comes along.

**Does it work offline?**\
Solo, completely. In a team, numbers you count without a connection are held by the app and sent once you're back online. Keep the app open until then. Undo in a team needs a connection.

**I tapped by mistake. Now what?**\
Tap Undo in the notice or under "Recently seen". In a team you can undo your own numbers, and the admin can undo any of them.

**We've been playing for a while. Do I have to start at 1?**\
No. Long-press the license plate and enter the number you're looking for. In a team only the admin can do this, because the number applies to everyone.

**What do my teammates see?**\
Your first name or nickname, and when you counted which number. Nobody outside your teams sees your name.
