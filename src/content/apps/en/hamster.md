Hamster keeps track of what's in your fridge, freezer and pantry, and when it expires. It's for anyone who throws food away because they missed the date, and for families and roommates who share their groceries. You can use it without an account. You only need one to share your pantry or use it on more than one device.

## Features

- **Expiry dates at a glance:** The home screen shows expired items in red and everything expiring within the next 14 days in orange.
- **Several dates per item:** Three yogurts with three dates stay one entry, and you check each one off as you use it.
- **Your own places:** Set up places like the fridge or the cellar with a color and icon, and see what's stored in each one.
- **Reminders:** At 9 am you get a notification if something expires within the next 1 to 30 days. Your phone schedules them itself, no server involved.
- **Shopping list:** Flagged items move to the list when you check them off, and you can move purchases back into your pantry with a new date.
- **Shared household:** With an account, you invite family or roommates with a six-character code. Everyone sees the same pantry and the same shopping list.

## How it works

1. Type a name, pick the expiry date and, if you like, a place.
2. The home screen shows what has expired and what expires soon.
3. When you use something up, check it off. If the item is flagged for the shopping list, it goes there.
4. To share, create an account and invite others to your household with the code.

## FAQ

**Can I download Hamster yet?**\
Not from the App Store yet. Hamster is in beta and is being prepared for the App Store.

**Do I need an account?**\
No. Without an account, Hamster keeps everything on your device. You only need one to sync or share a household.

**Where is my data stored if I have an account?**\
On servers in the EU, in Ireland, and it's only used for syncing. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and you can delete your account in the app at any time.

**Is Hamster available for Android or iPad?**\
The first version is iPhone only. There's no date yet for Android or an iPad layout.

**Which languages does Hamster support?**\
English, German and Spanish. It follows your device language, and you can switch in the settings.
