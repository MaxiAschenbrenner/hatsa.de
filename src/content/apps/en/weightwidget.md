WeightWidget is a weight log for iPhone built around its widgets. You log your weight in the morning straight from the Home Screen, without opening the app. Every entry goes into Apple Health, and there's no account to create.

## Features

- **Log from the widget:** The small, medium and large Home Screen widgets have plus, minus and save buttons.
- **Lock Screen:** Weight, distance to your goal and BMI each come as their own Lock Screen widget.
- **Trend and stats:** The chart covers a day, a week, a month, six months or a year, next to your change per week and month and a color-coded BMI.
- **Goal with forecast:** Set a goal weight to see your progress and an estimated arrival date based on your trend over the last 90 days.
- **Reminder and streak:** The daily reminder only shows up on days you haven't logged yet, and the app counts how many days in a row you've kept at it.
- **History and export:** All entries sit in one list that you can edit and export as a CSV file.

## How it works

1. Download the app and allow access to Apple Health. WeightWidget can't save anything without it.
2. Enter your current weight and put a widget on your Home or Lock Screen. The app walks you through it.
3. If you like, add your height for BMI, a goal weight and a time for the reminder.
4. From then on, a few taps on the widget each day are all it takes.

## FAQ

**How much does WeightWidget cost?**\
Nothing. The app is free, with no in-app purchases and no ads.

**Do I need an account?**\
No. There's no sign-in and no server the app talks to.

**Where is my data stored?**\
Weight, height and BMI live in Apple Health on your iPhone. Settings such as units, goal weight and reminder time sync between your devices through your own iCloud if iCloud is turned on.

**Which devices are supported?**\
iPhone with iOS 18 or later. There's no separate version for iPad or Apple Watch.

**Can I use pounds instead of kilograms?**\
Yes. In Settings you can switch between kg and lb, and between cm and ft/in for height. The app is available in English, German, Spanish and Portuguese.
